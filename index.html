Twenty slides. Twenty seconds each. How AI agents help us take the web back.

The wild west

Remember the early web?

Raw, weird, and utterly alive. Neon text, hit counters, MIDI tracks, and no algorithms telling us what to like.

The blank canvas

The digital renaissance

People built websites simply because they had something to share. The web felt vast, human, and unpredictable.

The consolidation

The wall comes up

We traded personal freedom for centralized feeds. The open web shrank inside the walled gardens of tech giants.

The sameness

The cookie-cutter web

Every brand looks identical, built for crawlers and engagement metrics rather than human souls. We lost our voice.

The trap

The algorithmic prison

Outrage got optimized, originality got punished, and creation became attention extraction. But the urge to build never died.

The gate

The barrier of code

Servers, databases, thousands of lines of boilerplate. The barrier kept brilliant non-technical creators locked out.

The shift

Enter the AI comrade

Not cheap replacements, powerful co-creators. The distance from an idea in your head to live on the web dropped to zero.

The mindset

Thirty years behind the decks

In DJing you curate, blend, and direct energy — you don't manufacture the instruments. Directing AI works the same way.
